NSE: RELIANCE · India · Energy, petrochemicals, retail and digital services · Company #1 in the website's provisional directory

5. Governance and assurance

FY25 BRSR reports 14 directors, including two women (14.29%). The BRSR identifies Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP in connection with assurance of selected sustainability disclosures. The assurance appendix and precise metric coverage must be checked before labeling any individual figure independently assured.

6. Climate strategy and financial risk

Reliance states a Net Carbon Zero by 2035 ambition. Its transition strategy includes new-energy investments and industrial decarbonisation initiatives. Ambition should not be confused with a verified forecast of achievement.

7. Research conclusions

  • Operational improvement: standalone Scope 1 emissions and physical-output emissions intensity decreased.
  • Area of concern: standalone Scope 2 emissions increased sharply and renewable share decreased.
  • Resource trade-off: water withdrawal increased even as water intensity improved.
  • Comparability constraint: standalone and O2C/E&P figures cannot be mixed.
  • Research gap: Scope 3, full waste indicators, site-level climate risks, financial sensitivity and indicator-level assurance mapping remain to be completed.

Method: Year-on-year change = (FY25 / FY24 − 1) × 100. Renewable share = renewable energy / total energy × 100. Calculated values are identified separately from company-reported figures. Reporting boundaries and assurance status should be rechecked before public release.
