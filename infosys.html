5. Climate Risk and Resilience

Physical climate risks

Infosys identifies potential climate-related physical risks including flooding, cyclones and wildfires.

Transition climate risks

Identified risk categories include emissions regulations, carbon pricing and increasing input costs associated with climate transition.

Climate strategy

Infosys reports climate-risk assessment and scenario-analysis activities, including long-term climate scenarios. It has also announced an ambition to become climate positive by 2030.
