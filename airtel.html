Research interpretation

Emissions: Standalone Scope 1 increased from 21,721 to 23,218 tCO₂e and Scope 2 from 470,775 to 490,367 tCO₂e. Combined emissions rose despite a decline in emissions intensity from 0.52 to 0.47 tCO₂e per INR million turnover.

Energy: Standalone renewable energy consumption rose from 5,963 GJ to 21,689 GJ, but remained less than 1% of total energy. The separately reported 49% renewable share of data-centre electricity has a different denominator and boundary.

People and governance: The dataset reports one employee fatality and four worker fatalities in FY2024–25, compared with zero and three respectively a year earlier. Female board representation was 3 of 10. These disclosures warrant context, not a simplistic ESG score.

Financial materiality: Telecom power costs, network resilience, data-centre efficiency, supplier emissions and worker safety are potential exposure channels. The supplied dataset does not establish quantified climate-related financial losses or a validated company-level financial-risk model.
