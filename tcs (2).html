Methodology and data gaps

The supplied research reports no named-site headcount, ownership, adaptation controls or quantified climate financial impacts. TCS's FY2025–26 report refers to a separate IFRS S2 scenario assessment, which was not included in this climate exposure ZIP. FY2026 observations are contextual, not FY2025 measurements.

To produce a defensible risk index, verify site coordinates, then overlay hazard datasets (flood, heat, water stress, cyclone, sea-level rise) and facility-level vulnerability and continuity controls. Distinguish disclosure status from independently confirmed hazard exposure.
