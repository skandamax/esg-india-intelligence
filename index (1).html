ESG essentials

What is ESG?

Environmental, Social and Governance factors help assess how organisations manage sustainability-related impacts, opportunities and risks.

Architects behind ESG

ESG evolved through many institutions. The UN-backed 2004 Who Cares Wins initiative helped popularise the term, and the Principles for Responsible Investment launched in 2006.

Why is ESG needed?

It supports informed capital allocation, risk management, accountability and understanding of impacts on people and the environment.

Reporting frameworks

Explore BRSR in India, GRI impact reporting and ISSB sustainability-related financial disclosures.

SEBI · GRI · IFRS Foundation

ESG analyst careers

Qualifications and skills

Finance, economics, environmental science, engineering and related degrees can provide entry routes. Data analysis, financial literacy and reporting knowledge are useful. Certifications such as CFA Institute Sustainable Investing and GARP SCR may complement experience.

What do ESG analysts do?

Collect and validate disclosures, assess material risks, compare companies, analyse scenarios and communicate findings.

Where are analysts employed?

Banks, asset managers, rating agencies, consultancies, corporate sustainability teams and research institutions.
